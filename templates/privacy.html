PRIVACY & LIMITS

Clear boundaries.

Your account is private.

Applications and resume text require your account session. We store password hashes and a hashed single-use recovery code. No email address is required. Operators with database administration access can technically access stored records; this is not end-to-end encryption.

Matching stays in this application.

No resume text is sent to an AI provider. The phrase matcher has a small skill dictionary and supports up to 30 custom phrases. It can miss synonyms and cannot reliably interpret negation, competence or seniority. Coverage is not a hiring probability or an employer ATS score.

Free, bounded storage.

100 accounts; 100 applications per account including archive; 20,000 characters per resume and job description; TXT import at most 80 KB; DOCX/text PDF at most 2 MB and 10 PDF pages. PDF parsing is isolated and bounded; OCR is unsupported. Upload files are not retained. Resume drafts and up to 10 saved searches are stored privately. Public job feeds contain employer listings, never account resumes. No application submission, email delivery or recruiter outreach.

Exports and deletion.

CSV exports application records; JSON includes your resume, application history, resume draft and saved searches. Calendar alerts require importing into your calendar. No automatic backup re-import. Account deletion requires your password and confirmation. Provider backups may retain older copies temporarily.

Job sources and eligibility.

We use twenty curated public employer boards through Greenhouse and Lever, not LinkedIn or Indeed scraping. LinkedIn, Indeed, Dice and ZipRecruiter buttons are external search links, not feed integrations. Opening one sends your entered keywords and location to that website; your resume and account records are not included. Refresh runs at deployment or a signed-in refresh request, with a 30-minute cooldown. No daily freshness guarantee. We retain cached jobs when feeds fail and show their last successful check. Removed public listings are purged after 30 days; tracked application copies are preserved. Authorization labels summarize explicit text, not legal eligibility. OPT, STEM OPT, H-1B, permanent residency and citizenship are never inferred from silence. Saved-search filters may reveal your interests; treat exports as private.

Availability.

Free hosting sleeps after inactivity and can take around a minute to wake. No uptime SLA, guaranteed continuous monitoring, independent security certification or Amazon-scale capacity is claimed. This is an AI-assisted portfolio project.
